The Wealth Gini Atlas harmonizes authoritative published wealth inequality series into a single long-format panel with explicit source priority and comparability metadata. Each row carries a comparability tier so you know whether you're looking at a survey-only estimate or one anchored to administrative records.

WID World Inequality Database · global Tier B
HFCS ECB Household Finance · Euro area Tier A
LWS Luxembourg Wealth Study · 24 countries Tier A
SCF Survey of Consumer Finances · US Tier A
DFA Fed Distributional Accounts · US quarterly Tier A
⬇︎

One file, five sources

WID · HFCS · LWS · SCF · DFA harmonized into a single Parquet or CSV with ISO-3 keys and CC BY 4.0 license. One pd.merge() away from any panel dataset.

⚙︎

Macro model calibration

The Moments Atlas companion supplies the exact targets heterogeneous-agent and HANK models need: top-1%, top-10%, bottom-50% shares and mean/median wealth for 53 countries, 1800–2025.

≋

Methodology-aware comparison

Every row carries comparability_tier (A = harmonized survey, B = mixed methodology) and top_tail_flag (survey-only vs admin-enhanced). Filter by quality in one line — no hand-curated exclusions.
