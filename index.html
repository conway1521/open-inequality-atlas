Ask about wealth inequality.

One question at a time. Answers come straight from a harmonized panel of 213 countries and territories, and each one adds a layer to the chart below. There is no model in the loop: questions are parsed, matched, and answered from the data, so every result is reproducible.

Your chain

    The panel

    Five source families reconciled into one long panel: WID, the ECB household survey (HFCS), the Luxembourg Wealth Study, the US Survey of Consumer Finances, and the Fed's Distributional Financial Accounts.

    10,783 rows with a non-null Gini. ISO3 keys, explicit source priority, 1800 to 2025 in the release files.

    Quality is a column

    Every row carries comparability_tier (A for harmonized surveys, B for mixed methodology) and top_tail_flag (survey only, or enhanced with administrative data).

    Filtering to like-for-like rows is one line of pandas, not a hand-curated exclusion list.

    What the box reads

    The question box covers the WID Gini series (1990 to 2024, per-adult equal-split, negative wealth zeroed), latest distribution shares by country, and the US long-run series back to 1989.

    The release files go further back and carry the full metadata. If you are doing research, take the files.
